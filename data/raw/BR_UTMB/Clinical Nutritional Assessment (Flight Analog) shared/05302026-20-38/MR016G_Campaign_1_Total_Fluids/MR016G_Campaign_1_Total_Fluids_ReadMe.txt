About This Data

This is a compilation of total fluid volume intake (in grams) 
for Campaign 1 subjects.  The non-meal fluid intake was derived 
from the CRF data.  The meal fluid intake is provided by UTMB 
Nutrition Lab & JSC Nutrition Lab in the subjects' daily dietary 
records.  The JSC nutrition lab then incorporates both non-meal & 
meal fluids into their overall dietary data that is submitted to 
LSDA.  The total fluid data here has been extracted from the individual 
subject records and put into multi-subject format per request of FAP for 
ease of use in writing their manuscript.
