9947
A - Test performed by ARUP Laboratories.
B - Osteocalcin by electrochemiluminescent immunoassay.  In patients with renal failure the osteocalcin result can be elevated, both directly, due to impaired clearance and indirectly, due to renal osteodystrophy.
C - Liver alkaline phosphatase can affect the measurement of bone specific alkaline phosphatase in this assay.  Each 100 U/L of liver alkaline phosphatase contributes an additional 2.5 to 5.8 ug/L to the bone specific alkaline phosphatase result.
D - Verified by repeat analysis.
E - Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR) has equal predictive value to Creatinine Clearance as suggested by the National Kidney Foundation.  This calculation applies to patients 18 years of age and older.  Calculated values are for Caucasians only; if the patient is African American multiply value by 1.21.  For more calculations refer to the National Kidney Disease website located at: http://www.nkdep.nih.gov/professionals/gfr_calculators.
